# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Startletes is committed to a welcoming, respectful community. We want participation to be safe and constructive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role in the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus discussion on ideas and work, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish real athlete information, private documents, or personal contact details.
- Follow the project's fake-data-only and mock-funding constraints.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing real athlete data, credentials, identity documents, or payment information in project materials.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a conduct concern, email **so.leeandrei25@gmail.com** privately with a description of what happened and, when relevant, links or other context. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. The maintainers will review reports confidentially, respond as promptly as practical, and take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning, removal of content, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the Startletes project in public.
