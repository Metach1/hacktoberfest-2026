# Community guidelines

Startlete is a community for athletes and supporters, including young people.
Treat every member with respect and keep shared content safe for all ages.

Do not post sexually explicit content, nude or sexualized images, links to adult
sites or paid adult-content pages, or anything that sexualizes or suggests
sexual activity involving a minor. Violations lead to content removal and
account suspension.

Use the report control on a post or image when something needs moderator
attention. Images held for review stay hidden until an admin approves them.
